## EmiMod

EmiMod develops methods for recording, modelling and assessing emissions from livestock buildings. The joint project addresses diffuse area sources such as naturally ventilated cattle and pig housing, outdoor yards and slurry stores. It considers ammonia, greenhouse gases, odour and bioaerosols and seeks simpler, purpose-specific measurement and assessment methods.

According to ATB, EmiMod runs from July 2023 to September 2028, is coordinated by KTBL and includes ATB and other research and public-sector partners. This repository addresses a complementary technical problem: preserving and harmonising the heterogeneous data needed to evaluate such measurement workflows reproducibly.

Official project description: https://www.atb-potsdam.de/en/research/research-programs/en-integriertes-reststoffmanagement/projects/project/projekt/emimod
