01 · Story

My work follows one chain: safe food, healthy animals, reliable measurements and climate decisions that can be audited.

I began in dairy and food technology, testing milk and food products for trace contaminants and adulteration. An M.Sc. in Dairy Science at Kiel moved my attention upstream—to feed analytics, ruminant nutrition, precision livestock farming and the first sensor systems that connect animal, environment and production data.

That path led to my PhD at Leibniz ATB Potsdam and Freie Universität Berlin. I now work on the precision and scalability of emission measurements in livestock buildings: automated 24/7 monitoring, high-resolution data pipelines and methods that remain robust outside the laboratory.

My instrument experience spans LC-MS/MS and GC-MS/MS, FTIR, CRDS, TDLAS and PAS, together with miniaturised NDIR, electrochemical and MOS sensor platforms. I build the surrounding system too—SOPs, QA protocols, metadata, validation rules and reproducible analysis.
